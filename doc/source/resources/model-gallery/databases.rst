Database lineage
================

A shared database connects studies without making their assumptions, code or results interchangeable. The editions on each study page identify the inputs actually documented by its authors.

EXIOBASE Hybrid
---------------

Historical starting point for the steel, PV and FULFILL workflows. Each study applies its own aggregation, extensions and reference years.

`Source and data <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10148587>`__

Green steel extensions
----------------------

The EU steel study develops into broader steel and hydrogen extensions. The GCAM example pins version 1.3.0; the documented PV reconstruction uses version 1.3.2.

`Source and data <https://zenodo.org/records/18744457>`__

NXSUT
-----

A maintained hybrid supply-use database with updated electricity and steel modelling. Version 3.0 is a possible background for future editions; no study in this gallery is claimed to have been reproduced on it.

`Source and data <https://zenodo.org/records/21638002>`__

Relationships between studies
-----------------------------

The 2024 EU steel study introduces technology extensions to EXIOBASE Hybrid. Later Green steel releases broaden those extensions. The GCAM–MARIO workflow uses the steel modelling line; the PV model adds its own supply chain and regional aggregation. FULFILL develops a separate preparation from EXIOBASE Hybrid.

`PV archive: documented relationship to Green steel <https://zenodo.org/records/20734983>`__ · `NXSUT methods and source <https://github.com/eNextHub/nxsut>`__

Future editions using NXSUT
---------------------------

NXSUT is a candidate shared background for future development. Such an edition would need its own assumptions and comparison with reference results. Historical study releases remain available with their original inputs.
